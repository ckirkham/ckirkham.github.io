# Kiwi Data

<p class="project-disciplines">Head of Product Design · UX/UI · AI Products</p>

I owned UX and UI across AI document-analysis and contract-intelligence products for Kiwi Data, an enterprise AI company serving more than eight Fortune 500 clients.

The work spanned document-review workflows, an annuity-adviser comparison experience and a contract builder. My role combined product design direction with detailed UX and UI, translating complex information and multi-step processes into coherent experiences for reviewing documents, comparing financial products and building contracts.
