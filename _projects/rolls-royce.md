# Rolls-Royce

<p class="project-disciplines">UX/UI, Interaction Design</p>

At AKQA, I designed UX and UI for the Rolls-Royce website and worked on UX, UI and interaction design for its configurator.

The projects covered two parts of the brand’s digital experience: presenting the brand online and helping customers explore vehicle customisation. The configurator work involved multi-screen journeys for use in dealerships and remotely.

My contribution connected experience structure, interface detail and interaction design across the website and configurator work, within the wider agency and client teams.

I designed UX and UI for the Rolls-Royce website at AKQA. The project was Highly Commended for Website Design at The Drum Design Awards 2016 and shortlisted for Web Design and Build at the 2016 BIMA Awards.
