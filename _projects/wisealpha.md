# WiseAlpha

<p class="project-disciplines">Product Design, Design Systems, AI-assisted Development</p>

I led UX, design and product delivery across WiseAlpha’s corporate-bond platform, covering trading, the client portal and B2B workflows for wealth managers and market makers.

The work spanned investment and bond-product management, pricing workflows and design systems. It also included Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank’s white-label Smart Sukuk experience and digital design language, developed with the bank’s design team.

I built WiseAlpha’s React Native mobile app using AI-assisted development and created an AI knowledge system connecting project knowledge, architecture decisions and delivery tickets across mobile, web and design systems.

WiseAlpha won Best Investments Provider at the 2019 British Bank Awards.
